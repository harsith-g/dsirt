Examination Instructions

Ph.D Entrance Examination — please read carefully before proceeding

How the examination works

  1. Paper 1 — Research Methodology: 50 MCQ questions, 60 minutes.
  2. After you submit Paper 1 (or its time ends) there is a 15-minute break. You will be signed out.
  3. After the break, sign in again, choose your domain and take Paper 2 — Domain Specific: 50 MCQ questions, 60 minutes.
  4. Each correct answer carries 1 mark. There is no negative marking.
  5. Your answer is saved automatically the moment you tap an option. The green "Saved" sign at the top confirms it.
  6. When a phase's time runs out, your saved answers are submitted automatically.
  7. Once a phase is submitted, its answers cannot be changed.
  8. Use the question numbers (navigator) to jump to any question, and "Mark for review" to flag questions you want to revisit.
  9. If your internet drops, keep working — answers are kept on your device and sent as soon as the connection returns. If the page closes, simply sign in again: you continue where you stopped, with the same time.
  10. Your camera must stay on during both phases.

During the examination

  • Do not switch tabs, open other apps or minimise the browser.
  • On a computer, stay in full-screen mode.
  • Do not copy examination content. Right-click is disabled.
  • These events are recorded as warnings for the examiner to review. They never submit your exam.

Results

  • Results are processed by the examination administrator and are not shown during the exam.
