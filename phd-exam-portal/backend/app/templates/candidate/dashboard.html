Examination instructions

Read these carefully. They are enforced by the system, not by request.

Structure

  • Phase 1 — Research Methodology (common to all candidates), {{ exam.phase1_duration_mins if exam else 60 }} minutes.
  • Phase 2 — your chosen domain, {{ exam.phase2_duration_mins if exam else 60 }} minutes, timed and scored separately.
  • After submitting Phase 1 you choose exactly one domain. That choice is locked the moment you save it and cannot be changed.

Timing

  • The clock runs on the server. Closing the browser, losing your connection or changing your device clock does not pause it.
  • When the time expires your paper is submitted automatically with whatever has been saved.
  • Every answer is saved automatically a moment after you select it.

Proctoring

  • Your webcam must stay on for the whole examination and is streamed live to a proctor.
  • The paper runs in full screen. Leaving full screen, switching tabs or switching windows is recorded as a violation with a timestamped snapshot.
  • Right-click, text selection, copy, paste and developer shortcuts are disabled.
  • Repeated violations are flagged to the examination cell for review.

Marking

  • Each question carries 1 mark. There is no negative marking.
  • Questions and options are presented in a different order for each candidate.
  • Unanswered questions score zero; they are never penalised.
