Examination Instructions

Ph.D Entrance Examination — Please read carefully before proceeding

General Instructions

  1. The examination consists of two phases:
    • Phase 1 — Research Methodology: 50 MCQ questions.
    • Phase 2 — Domain Specific: 50 MCQ questions from your chosen department.
  2. Each correct answer carries 1 mark. There is no negative marking.
  3. You must complete Phase 1 and explicitly submit it before you can start Phase 2.
  4. Once a phase is submitted, answers in that phase cannot be changed.
  5. The timer shown is the time remaining until the global exam end time. A late start means less available time — plan accordingly.
  6. Answers are saved automatically after every selection. Do not close the browser during the exam.
  7. You may navigate freely within the current phase using the question navigator.
  8. Use "Mark for Review" to flag questions you wish to revisit before submitting.
  9. Candidates must remain in full-screen mode throughout the examination.
  10. Your camera must remain active throughout the examination for live proctoring.

During the Examination

  • Do not switch tabs, open new windows, or minimize the browser.
  • Do not use the keyboard shortcut to open developer tools.
  • Do not attempt to copy examination content.
  • Right-clicking is disabled during the examination.
  • All violations are automatically logged and reviewed by the examination administrator.

Results

  • Results will be processed by the examination administrator.
  • Scores, correct answers, and result details will not be displayed to candidates.
  • Only the examination administrator has access to result information.

Monitoring Rules

The following events are monitored and recorded by the examination system

⚠️
Tab Switching or Window Change

If you navigate away from the examination tab, this is recorded as an infraction.

⚠️
Exiting Full-Screen Mode

Leaving full-screen is recorded as an infraction. You will be prompted to return.

⚠️
Copying Exam Content

Any attempt to copy text from the examination is blocked and recorded.

⚠️
Right-Click Menu

The right-click context menu is disabled throughout the examination.

📹
Camera Monitoring

Your camera is active throughout the examination. The live feed is visible to the examination administrator.

Important: All infractions are reviewed by a human administrator. Violations are recorded as warnings only. The examination system does not automatically submit or end your session for any infraction. Only the administrator can take such action.
